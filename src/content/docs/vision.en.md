# Foundational principles

The development of advanced AI poses existential risk to humanity, akin to the risks of nuclear weapons, pandemics and climate change, if not greater. Current development of AI does not give safety enough priority, and the lives of everyone on Earth are being gambled with by a few politicians and business leaders who hold most of the power. This is an unfair and dangerous state of the world that needs to be put right.

# Mission

To give Portugal a voice in the development of advanced AI so that it is safe, aligned to humans and equitable. We are part of a larger global effort of AI safety communities working on public awareness, technical research and international policy and governance. We aim to address the broad range of societal harms from transformative AI, from job loss and deepfakes to catastrophic loss of life and extinction.

# Our role

Portugal is home to a highly educated youth population, a thriving tech sector, and a large community of expats, digital nomads, remote workers and immigrants who chose Portugal for its weather and way of life. Portugal is also a member of the EU and a close ally of the UK and other Western nations.

The talent and curiosity are strong, and the potential to make a difference is there. But without a community that acts as a unified voice, not much can be done. LAISH is the community that brings together people from all over Portugal to work on AI safety problems and campaign for a more just future.

# Our vision

We aim to make AI safety a priority in Portugal. We educate people on the risks and capabilities of AI, steer them towards work or volunteering for organisations that work on AI safety, encourage them to research AI safety topics, and spread the message so that AI safety is a concern for all citizens. We believe every nation can do this and that it can become a worldwide movement. We also believe that EU member states have an outsized impact through their regulatory power, and that ideas which start at national level can spread to the EU through policy proposals and discussions among politicians and parliamentarians.

# Actions

- **Community events:** dialogue on AI safety that brings people living in Portugal together.
- **Courses:** structured courses on the fundamentals of AI safety.
- **Career advising:** individual feedback and guidance for moving into AI safety roles.
- **International connections:** linking the AI safety communities of Europe.
- **Outreach:** attending and speaking at external events and universities.
- **Talent pipeline:** helping people move along the talent pipeline (below).

# The talent pipeline

LAISH sees people as being somewhere on a pipeline. This is adapted largely from Safe AI Netherlands (SAIN), whose model we think is a strong one to follow.

| Level | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No engagement | Has not met AI safety |
| 1 | Initial engagement | Comes to a LAISH event, reads a Substack post, sees content on social media |
| 2 | Foundational learning | Completes a LAISH course, joins a discussion group |
| 3 | Active contribution | Joins a research project, takes part in a campaign, completes a hackathon |
| 4 | Structured development | A full-time, paid fellowship or internship in AI safety |
| 5 | Professional impact | Works full-time in AI safety, policy, research, industry or civil society |

At LAISH we focus on people at levels 0, 1 and 2 and help them take the next step. For example, someone at level 0 moves to level 1 when they come to a meetup and read our newsletter. Someone at level 2 moves to level 3 after our Foundations of AI Safety Fellowship.

# The future

LAISH is still a young organisation with a lot of room to grow. Our plans:

- **More courses:** more places next semester, an extended fellowship and an intensive one-week course.
- **International hackathons:** joint events with the AI safety communities in Barcelona and Madrid, to give people something real to work on and build collaboration across the Iberian Peninsula.
- **Political outreach:** a campaign asking Portuguese politicians to commit to the development of safe AI.
- **A permanent home:** a space for research collaboration and more events.

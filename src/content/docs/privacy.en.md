# Who we are

Lisbon AI Safety Hub (LAISH) is a volunteer-run initiative in Lisbon. Until LAISH is registered as an association, the person responsible for your data is Caleb Price. Contact: [contact@lisbonaisafetyhub.org](mailto:contact@lisbonaisafetyhub.org)

# What we collect

- Your name and email address
- For some activities, your phone number and Discord handle
- Which events you registered for and attended
- How you heard about us
- For fellowship applicants, your application answers
- If you subscribe to our Substack, the information Substack shares with publishers, including your approximate country

This website has no forms and sets no cookies. [Confirm: if Cloudflare Web Analytics is turned on, it counts page views without cookies and without storing your IP address.]

# Why we use it, and our legal basis

- To run events you sign up for and keep attendance records so we can plan and report on our work. This is based on our legitimate interest.
- To run the fellowship and send you session reminders. This is necessary to deliver the programme you joined.
- To email you about future events, only if you opted in. You can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us.

# Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your data. We use these services to run LAISH:

- Luma and Meetup (event registration)
- Airtable (our contact list)
- Substack (newsletter)
- Google Workspace (email and documents)
- Discord (community)
- Notion (internal planning)
- Granola (meeting notes)
- Cloudflare (website hosting and email routing)

Some of these services are based in the United States. For those transfers we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

# How long we keep it

- Attendance records: 24 months after your last event
- Waitlist-only registrations: 12 months after the event
- Fellowship applications: 12 months after the selection decision
- Fellows' phone numbers: 6 months after the cohort ends
- Event email list: until you unsubscribe

# Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Email [contact@lisbonaisafetyhub.org](mailto:contact@lisbonaisafetyhub.org) and we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to Portugal's data protection authority, the CNPD: [www.cnpd.pt](https://www.cnpd.pt)
